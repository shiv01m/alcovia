Rules

  1. Drop when cost per result is above the threshold multiple of the median for that campaign type.
  2. Refresh when frequency hits the cap, or CTR has fallen by the decay threshold since the campaign's first 14 days.
  3. Scale when cost per result is at or below the median and neither refresh signal is on.
  4. Hold otherwise. Result = qualified lead for program ads, lead for workshop ads.

Thresholds

Where each number comes from. Revenue: Razorpay captured payments (cash basis). Leads and stages: every ClickUp status change, written to the database by an n8n webhook. Ads: Meta Marketing API, pulled daily at 06:00 IST. Landing pages: GA4. Targets: set by the founders on the 1st of each month.
